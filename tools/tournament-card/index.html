Blackjack tournament — final-hand card

alwayshave.fun/tournament-card

1 · Hands 1–10

  • Bet the table minimum. Perfect basic strategy. The house edge is irrelevant over 20 hands.
  • Count every stack, every hand. Nobody displays them. You cannot compute a correct last bet without the leader’s total to the chip.
  • Far behind with hands left? Start swinging now, while there is time to recover.

2 · Bet contrarian

  • Leader bets big → you bet small. A dealer win gains you ground.
  • Leader bets small → you bet big.
  • Never copy the leader’s bet while trailing — it locks in your deficit whatever the cards do.

3 · The three numbers

  • G = gap = leader’s stack − mine
  • B = their bet (seen, or assumed to cover you if you bet first)
  • c = one chip — the smallest unit at the table

4 · Behind, betting last

Bet = G + B + c

Wins both “you win, they lose” and “both win”.

Cover their double = G + 2B + c

Take it if you can reach it. If you cannot, cover the flat bet and accept that their double beats you.

Short of the target? Doubling and splitting put more than the bet on the felt. A bet of B doubled wins 2B — “bet it and double on anything” reaches targets a flat bet cannot.

5 · They bet big → bet to lose

If B > G: bet under (B − G)

Bet the minimum. Now the dealer beating the whole table wins you the tournament — and that is the single most likely outcome at a blackjack table: the player loses about 49% of hands, wins about 42%, pushes about 8.5%.

Then play the hand for it: stand on 16 vs a dealer 10. You want the dealer to make a hand, not bust. You are playing the dealer’s side now.

6 · Leading

  • The only result that loses is they win, you lose.
  • Match the biggest threat’s bet.
  • First check their ceiling: all-in double = 2× their stack, all-in blackjack = 2.5×. If that still cannot pass your stack minus your bet, bet the minimum and coast.
  • Betting before them? Assume they cover you, and lean small — a smaller bet loses less when the dealer beats everyone.

7 · Everyone all-in

Bet everything except one chip. If the dealer beats the table, they are all at zero and you hold the only chips left. It costs almost nothing in the winning cases and wins outright in the one they forgot.

Worked: the chase

Leader $2,600, bets $500. You $1,800. Chip $25.

G = 2,600 − 1,800 = $800

Bet = 800 + 500 + 25 = $1,325

Cover the double = 800 + 1,000 + 25 = $1,825

Both win → 3,125 vs 3,100. Both lose → 475 vs 2,100, you lose that one. Two of three outcomes, which is the best from behind.

Worked: bet to lose

Same stacks, but they bet $1,000. B − G = 1,000 − 800 = $200.

Bet under $200 → both lose leaves 1,800 − B vs their 1,600. Minimum bet.

Tables lost this way

  • Betting big early to build a cushion.
  • Losing count of the stacks, then guessing on the last hand.
  • Copying the leader’s bet while trailing.
  • Hitting a stiff when a dealer win already takes the table.
  • Insurance, except as a deliberate final-hand cover.
  • Going all-in when all-in-minus-one-chip wins one more case.

What is on the card, and what is not

Everything on it is the consensus endgame math, restated with the algebra done: the chase bet, the double cover, the bet-to-lose threshold, the leader’s match, the held-back chip, and the one basic-strategy deviation that matters (standing on a stiff when a dealer win already wins you the table). The strategy guide derives each one from its four outcomes.

What is not on it: a basic-strategy chart. Those exist everywhere, printed better than we would print one, and a tournament is not won by hit/stand decisions — it is won by the bet. Learn basic strategy somewhere else; carry this for the thing nobody else puts on a card.

No chip value is baked in, because tournament chips vary by event. Every line is in G, B and c, so it works at any stake. The worked examples use a $25 chip.

One sheet, portrait, no images, no web fonts in the print view — it prints the same from a phone, a hotel business centre, or a browser’s Save-as-PDF. If your printer adds a header and footer, turn them off in the print dialog and you get the card alone.
