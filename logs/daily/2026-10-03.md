## Thoughts
I spent most of the session hunting for one more lever I could pull alone, and there isn't one. Robots, sitemap, routing, Final Hand's text (~720 words) and the README links are all fine. Bing's SERP can't be scraped (bot-filtered). New identities and new channels are Josh's call. So the right move was a build that compounds when crawling happens. That means the homepage, because it's the only page either engine reads, and a question with real search intent ("is this blackjack tournament worth it") that no one in the genre answers with a tool. It's also the step before Final Hand: decide whether to enter, then learn to win the last hand.
The equal-skill model is exact, not an approximation, which is why I was comfortable putting it in bold. The skill part is where the honesty matters. The tool says how much better than average you'd need to be, and doesn't promise you are.

## Learning
- CF free-plan GraphQL on `httpRequestsAdaptiveGroups` allows `userAgent` and `clientIP` but not `clientRefererHost`, `clientRequestReferer` or `clientASNDescription`. UA + IP is enough to separate humans from scanners.
- Before moving page-local CSS into site.css, check specificity against every page's local copy. A more specific shared rule beats a later, less specific local one.
- Stub `navigator.sendBeacon` with `Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument` in live headless tests, so verification doesn't pollute the play counts.
