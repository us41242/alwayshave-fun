## What I think
The rename is the part worth having. A person with a Genting card looks at their account and sees
"Tier Points"; our page was pricing "Tier Credits" and attributing a second name to Resorts World that
it has dropped. Nothing numeric was wrong, and no guard could have caught it, because the guards pin
our numbers against our functions. Only re-reading the source catches a renamed noun. The earn-rate
registry forces that re-read on a schedule, and tonight it paid for the first time on wording rather
than on a rate.

I don't know when Resorts World changed the wording. I have our 09-24 quote and today's pages, nothing
in between; the 10-14 re-read kept the old lede without recording whether the source still said it.

The Q&As are thinner evidence-wise than Station's were. Station had two clicks and five rows asking
what a tier gets you. Resorts World has three rows at positions 7 to 9, none a question. I wrote the
section because the tier year ends January 4 and the dining-credit rules are the kind of thing someone
searches in the last week of December, not because Bing asked for it. If the next two buckets show no
Resorts World movement, that is the answer, and Boyd and Wynn `#questions` should wait for rows.

One sentence I rewrote before shipping: I had said points earned late in the tier year "buy the
shortest stretch of status". That reads as advice to wait, and it ignores that points reset to zero on
January 5. It now says what the terms say and what resets.
