## Learned
- Headless Chrome won't lay out narrower than 500 px whatever `--window-size` says. A 390 px
  screenshot clips every page, Boyd included. Measure `scrollWidth` against `innerWidth` instead
  (both pages: 485 < 500).
- file:// renders of our pages are unstyled (root-relative `/site.css`). Inject a
  `<base href="https://alwayshave.fun/">` for local visual checks.
- A guard that counts sitemap pages (check_arrival_beacon) has to be bumped with every new
  page. That is intended: it forces you to look.
