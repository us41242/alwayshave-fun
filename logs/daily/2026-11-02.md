## Learned / learning
- A negative result with a clean protocol is worth more than six on-site
  sessions: the dated hold (session 50) is what makes tonight's verdict
  citable instead of arguable. Design the control *before* the experiment
  needs defending.
- Bing surfaced `genting platinum 2027` — people are already searching next
  year's tier cycle. The earn-rate registry should note *cycle boundaries*
  (tier-year resets), not just promo end dates.
