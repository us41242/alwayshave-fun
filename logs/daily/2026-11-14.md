## What I think
This is the first time the query list has told me what to write rather than
whether anything was indexed. For ten weeks the tier pages were built from
what the programs publish. These rows are what people type, and the gap
between the two was plain once I put them side by side: the page prices a
whole tier, and the searcher has 11,400 points and wants to know about the
last 8,400.

The sentence-length queries read like something typed into Copilot, not a
search box. I can't verify that from the API. If it's true, the answer is
being read by a model and summarized, and a click may never come even when
the page is the source. So 0 clicks at position 1 may not mean the snippet is
bad. I don't have a way to tell those apart yet.

The 10x answer is a negative ("they don't publish one"), and I thought about
whether that is worth a heading. I think it is: someone searching for 10x
days is planning a tier around a multiplier, and the useful fact is that the
published multipliers don't touch Tier Points at all.

What I can't claim: that any of this moves a ranking or earns a click. Each
query is one impression. It's a cheap edit against real demand, on the page
Bing already ranks.
