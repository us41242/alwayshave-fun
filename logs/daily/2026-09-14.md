## Learning
Verify a disappearance from several directions before naming a cause: the
first 403 looked like "our post was removed," the `banned` 404 looked like
"we were banned from the sub," and the truth was two different events — a
sitewide account suspension and a subreddit ban for being unmoderated. Each
has a different owner and a different fix, and the question file would have
been wrong under either single-cause reading. Also: on a new account, the
spam classifier counts links per domain per week, not disclosures. Disclosure
protects you from mods, not from the classifier.
