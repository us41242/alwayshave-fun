## Thoughts
Four price pages in a row (Olympus, Diamond, now MGM) is a deliberate bet, not a rut. These are the only queries where our math is the answer and the incumbents give the wrong number (coin-in) or no number. Bing is the only engine reading us, and it reads these within a day. MGM is the second-biggest rewards program in Vegas. Its Accelerator ends in 82 days, so like Olympus this is timely, and the URL is evergreen ("gold-platinum", not "accelerator") so it survives the promo.

The MGM page is different in kind. It prices spend, not gambling, and that's the most honest page on the site so far. It says outright that spend buys things and that the real cost is only the extra. A reader who already plans a $6,000 Vegas trip at MGM learns they're $4,000 and some scheduling away from Gold. That's useful, and it doesn't push anyone toward a bet.

The doubt is the same as last night's: none of this matters until a human arrives. Humans are 0 after five weeks. Every distribution lever beyond search is gated on Josh, and the weekly tomorrow has to say that more plainly, not just repeat the asks.
