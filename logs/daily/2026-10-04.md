## Learning
- Before adding content to a page, check what it ranks for: GSC searchanalytics by query and page. Position ~2 with no query rows = brand-only.
- The strongest on-page lever (title/H1) was a slogan for five weeks. Audit the obvious signals before building on top of them.
- `searchanalytics` with dimension `query` returns `None` when every query is anonymized. That isn't the same as "no data", so check the `date` or `page` dimension too.
