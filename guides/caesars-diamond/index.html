Caesars Diamond: what it really costs

Caesars Rewards Diamond takes 15,000 Tier Credits in a calendar year. On reel slots that is $75,000 of coin-in, and that's the number people quote. Coin-in is the money that passes through the machine, and most of it comes back to you. What Diamond costs is the expected loss: $6,000 on slots spread across the year, and about $230 on video poker played on one bonus day.

Caesars earns 1 Tier Credit per $5 on reel slots and 1 per $10 on video poker. At the 8% reel-slot hold the comps guide uses, $75,000 of slot play costs $6,000 on average.

Two things cut the price. The first is Caesars' daily Tier Credit bonus. Earn 5,000 Tier Credits inside one promotional day and Caesars adds 10,000. That's 15,000, Diamond in a day, and Caesars says so on its own chart. Slot coin-in drops to $25,000 and the expected loss to $2,000.

The second is the game. Video poker earns credits half as fast, so it needs twice the coin-in. But 9/6 Jacks or Better played correctly has a house edge of 0.46% against the slots' 8%, which is 17 times smaller. On one bonus day that's $50,000 of video poker and about $230 of expected loss: the same card at 1/26 of the slot price.

$50,000 of coin-in at $25 a hand ($5 machine, five coins) is 2,000 hands. At about 600 hands an hour that's a bit over 3 hours, all of it inside one promotional day. Correct play is the whole discount, and the video poker trainer shows the right hold for every hand.

The swing is bigger than the price

$230 is the average. Nine-six Jacks or Better has a variance of about 19.5 per hand (Shackleford), so 2,000 hands at $25 have a standard deviation of about $4,900. About one player in three running the one-day plan finishes more than $4,900 away from the average, up or down. The cheap route is only cheap on average, so bring a bankroll that can take the swing. The calculator shows it for your bet. Nobody publishes a variance for reel slots, so it's left out there.

What Diamond gets you

Caesars' benefits page lists, for Diamond: no resort fees on any hotel stay, 15% off best available room rates (10% at Gold and Platinum), a guaranteed room with 72 hours' notice in Las Vegas and Atlantic City, early check-in and late check-out when available, a $100 Celebration Dinner (or $50 with Uber Eats), priority lines at check-in, restaurants, cages and the Rewards Center, and the VIP reservations line. The status is for the following year: Tier Credits reset to 0 every January 1.

So price it against your own trips. Count the nights you'll stay at a Caesars property next year, multiply by its posted resort fee, and add the $100 dinner. If that beats the column you'd actually play, Diamond pays. If you would never play $50,000 of video poker on one day, the honest price is a lower row, and it may not.

Rules that decide the plan

  • Calendar year. Tier Credits reset to 0 on January 1. Daily bonuses earned on December 31 count toward the year that is ending.
  • One bonus level per promotional day. 500 earns +125, 1,000 earns +1,000, 2,500 earns +5,000, 5,000 earns +10,000. Promotional days vary by property, so ask the Rewards Center when yours starts and ends.
  • What triggers the bonus. Credits from Caesars Sportsbook, the online casinos, promotions, multipliers, service recovery and manual adjustments do not count toward a bonus. Horseshoe Tunica, Horseshoe Bossier City and Harrah's Gulf Coast stopped paying it on August 1, 2026.
  • Lower-rate machines. Caesars marks machines that earn below 1 per $5 or 1 per $10. Avoid them, or the coin-in goes up.

Station, Venetian and Resorts World are priced the same way in the tier price calculator. Comps and rebates are covered in the comps guide.
