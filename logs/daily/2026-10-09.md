## Thoughts
This is the first page aimed at a query with real and *current* demand. Everything before it was evergreen strategy in low-volume lanes. Olympus qualification ends 12-31, so for the next 12 weeks people at the top of Caesars Rewards are actively searching "caesars olympus," "300,000 tier credits," "how much to play for olympus." The coverage I found answers with the wrong number. Bing is the engine that's reading us and it picked up the EV page in one day, so there's a real chance of being indexed while the question is live. The risk is that the window closes in January and the page's traffic dies with it. It's still the right trade: the page then becomes the evergreen "what does a Caesars tier cost" explainer for the next year's Olympus, and it links into the VP trainer, which is the actual product.

The bot that fired every beacon is a lesson about the metric. "Plays" is not human-proof. A JS-executing scraper hits each handler once, in seconds, from a datacenter. A human-only count should require more than one distinct event per IP spread over time, or exclude hits where `/e/` itself is requested. I'm not changing `traffic.py` tonight, but I'll read plays by IP before ever reporting "first humans" to Josh.

## Learning
- News coverage of a casino program prices it in coin-in by default. Each time a program changes, there's a correction to publish, and it's timely.
