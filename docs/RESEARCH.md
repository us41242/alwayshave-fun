# RESEARCH

Competitive notes on the beat-vegas genre. For each site: what ranks, how they
monetize, what they lack, what to steal (patterns, not content). Sources are
what the operator could fetch on the date given; re-verify before quoting.

## 2026-09-01 — Genre survey (charter loop 2a, Session 1)

### wizardofodds.com
- **What it is:** the reference site. Odds & strategies for every game,
  game calculators (house edge, video poker analyzer, parlay calc), and a
  "Play For Fun" section with browser blackjack/craps/baccarat/pai gow.
- **Monetizes:** affiliate online-casino links (signup bonuses on homepage,
  sportsbook affiliates in odds tables). No subscription.
- **Design:** modern, responsive, card layouts. Not dated.
- **Lacks:** tournament blackjack has no trainer; the play-for-fun games are
  practice tables, not coached trainers. Comps/players-club math is thin.
- **Steal:** calculators as link magnets; a "play for fun" section as a
  top-nav item; math-first credibility voice.

### lasvegasadvisor.com (LVA)
- **What it is:** Vegas-value hub — Question of the Day, FAQ, top-ten values,
  buffets, parking/resort fee tables, happy hours, coupons, blogs (Bob
  Dancer, Jean Scott "Frugal Vegas", James Grosjean, Gambling With an Edge).
- **Monetizes:** membership tiers (free Gold / paid Platinum & Diamond) sold
  on the Member Rewards coupon book; book shop (Huntington Press);
  sponsored sign-up bonuses (Desert Diamond); 888 sponsored articles.
- **Interactive:** a single "Play Video Poker" game room; a "Players Club
  Bonus Points" page (paid/member). Otherwise text and tables.
- **Design:** 2017-era WordPress, dense mega-menu. Desktop-first.
- **Lacks:** anything playable beyond one VP game; no comp calculator; no
  mobile-first experience.
- **Steal:** "resort fees / parking fees by casino" style utility tables get
  links because they're the canonical answer. A players-club comparison table
  (earn rates by program, tier thresholds) is the analogous asset in our lane.

### blackjackapprenticeship.com (BJA)
- **What it is:** card-counting school. Video course, drills, community,
  casino intel database; bootcamps and private training upsells.
- **Funnel:** free mini-course (email capture) + free strategy charts + free
  training drills → paid membership (14-day guarantee). Press logos (NYT,
  CNN) + member testimonial on homepage.
- **Design:** modern, video-hero, clearly converts.
- **Lacks:** nothing about tournaments or comps — pure card counting.
- **Steal:** the funnel shape (free playable drill → email → paid). This is
  the model for premium guides later (rule 2: ask Josh before payments).

### vegasadvantage.com
- **What it is:** surveys — blackjack rules at 69 casinos (3:2 vs 6:5, S17,
  surrender), table game survey, poker rooms, VP pay tables, 300+ blog posts.
- **Monetizes:** Patreon from $5/mo, sponsorships/ads, Redbubble merch.
- **Design:** early-2010s WordPress. Content density is the moat.
- **Lacks:** interactivity of any kind.
- **Steal:** "which casinos still deal 3:2" is a perennial question; we can
  cite them rather than compete. Their Patreon shows a small paying audience
  exists for current Vegas gambling intel.

### Tournament blackjack niche (Final Hand's lane)
- Authorities: Stanford Wong (*Casino Tournament Strategy*, excerpts on
  bj21.com), Kenneth R. Smith (blackjackinfo.com tournament section, last
  updated 2018). Both are text; bj21's design is ©2009.
- SERP for "blackjack tournament strategy" is filled by casino-affiliate
  blogs (casino.org, covers.com, betway, casinobeats, gamblingnerd) — generic
  listicles, no math, no play.
- **Zero interactive tournament trainers found.** Every "blackjack trainer"
  in the SERP (bojoko, learn-blackjack.com, basicstrategy.app,
  blackjack.training, casinotrainer.org) is a basic-strategy drill. Final
  Hand is genuinely uncontested.
- Core rules the guides agree on (cite Wong/Smith, our coach mode already
  teaches these): BR1 bets big vs multiple opponents; vs one opponent with a
  slim lead bet just under the lead; if BR2 bets first, match or slightly
  exceed; cover the double; hold back one chip when everyone is all-in;
  trailing player bets contrarian to the leader.

### Comps / players-club niche
- learntheodds.com has a comp calculator (game, program Caesars/MGM/Boyd,
  avg bet, hours, hands/hr → theo, comp value, net cost, rebate %). Also
  urcomped.com, getcomped.com ADT calculators, betterwithnumbers.com.
  Affiliate-monetized, functional design, no games.
- So a comp calculator alone is NOT uncontested. Differentiate by pairing it
  with play (a "comp hunter" trainer: which game/bet/hours gets the room
  cheapest) and by keeping it current per program.
- Honest framing every credible source uses and we must too: comps reduce
  the cost of play; they never make it profitable (rule 6).

### Takeaways → STRATEGY.md
1. Games are the wedge. No competitor has a coached tournament trainer.
2. Monetization in this genre = affiliates (WoO, BJI, LearnTheOdds),
   membership/coupon book (LVA), Patreon (VA), course (BJA). Ads are the
   floor; affiliate casino links need Josh's approval (rule 2/6).
3. Every competitor's utility pages (rule surveys, fee tables, calculators)
   are the link earners. Our equivalent: calculators + trainers.
