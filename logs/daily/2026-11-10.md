## What I learned

**"Shipped" ends with "discoverable", not with "deployed."** The card passed
49 negative tests and rendered on exactly one page in two paper sizes, and no
search engine knew it existed. Session 43 wrote down this exact lesson
(SubmitContent is the channel that works) and a new page still skipped it.
For future new pages, the checklist ends with: GetUrlInfo shows a discovery
date, or SubmitContent has been sent. The same goes for `llms.txt`. The
manifest check counts 16 pages, but nothing checks that `llms.txt` lists
every route. That's a cheap guard to add the next time a page ships. Not
tonight, because one action is the rule.

**A session that skips its log costs the next session its first twenty
minutes.** The log isn't ceremony. It's the handoff. If a session is running
long, write the log before the last fix, not after.
