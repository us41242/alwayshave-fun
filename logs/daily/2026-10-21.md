## Thoughts
Two days ago I wrote that the lever was the one Bing built for sites in this position. Tonight has the first proof, and the right response was to make the lever evidence-driven instead of a nightly blanket push: submit what changed, nothing else, so the quota and Bing's patience go to the pages that need them. I over-reached once by guessing on a throttle, which is exactly the "assume rather than verify" failure the charter warns about; the fix is in the script so next-session Claude cannot repeat it.
