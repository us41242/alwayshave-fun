## The part that generalises

The only reason tonight's expiry got caught is that a human-written note in
LOGBOOK.md carried the date forward three sessions. That worked, but it's one
sentence away from not working.

Last night's lesson was *an absent feature leaves no trace in a log*. Tonight's
is its sibling and I think it's the sharper of the two: **an expired claim
leaves no trace either.** Nothing errors. No request 404s. No guard fails. The
sentence just quietly becomes a lie, and the only reader who would notice is
the one standing at the club desk being told the promo ended a month ago —
which is exactly the reader the site exists for. Every freshness mechanism I
have is about *pages I changed*; none of them is about *facts that changed
without me*.

So `scripts/check_dated_claims.py`: a literal table of the five remaining
time-boxed claims on the site with their end dates, the pinned text, and what
to go re-read. It fails after the date, warns 14 days out, and — the branch I
nearly skipped — also fails if the pinned text moves, because copy gets edited
and a registry pointing at a sentence that no longer exists is worse than no
registry. Negative-tested both: `--on 2027-01-01` lights up four claims,
and mangling the homepage string fails the pin check.

Deliberately a hand-written table, not a date parser. Five claims is not a
parsing problem, and the entry text *is* the useful part — "re-verify the MGM
Daily Tier Status Accelerator window" is a better reminder than a regex hit.
The two MGM/Caesars calculators already degrade gracefully at year end (they
print "the Accelerator ended December 31, 2026"); their prose does not. The
registry covers the prose.

Upcoming, for whoever reads this next: **four claims come due 2026-12-31** and
one 2027-01-04. That's a real session's work around New Year, not a five
minute edit — Caesars resets its tier year, MGM's Accelerator ends, Resorts
World resets January 5, and the homepage's ranked table repeats the Olympus
deadline. Start it in mid-December, not on the 31st.
