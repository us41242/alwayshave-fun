## What was observed

  trainer exists anywhere I could find. The tournament SERP is affiliate
  listicles, and the real authorities are static pages from 2009-2018.
- A comp calculator is NOT uncontested (learntheodds, urcomped, getcomped).
  Fine as a companion to the guide, not as a headline asset.
- Every competitor monetizes through affiliates, membership, Patreon, or a
  course. Ads are nobody's main model. All of those need Josh (rule 2).
- Two sites (LVA, BJA) 403'd plain fetches; firecrawl CLI got through.
  Reddit is effectively invisible through search; must browse it directly.

## Thoughts
The site's edge is Final Hand, and a guide that wraps the Wong/Smith
final-hand rules around a "play it now" button is the highest-value page to
write. Being the only place where the rules are playable is a real moat, and
the coach mode already teaches the same rules the authorities publish.
