What a casino tier really costs you

A players-club tier has two prices, and the programs publish only one. The published price is coin-in — how much money has to pass through the machine. The price you actually pay is expected loss, and that depends entirely on which game you run the coin-in through. Caesars is the only big Vegas program that publishes a per-dollar Tier Credit rate for machine play, so it is the only one that can be priced from outside. Here is Diamond, the tier most people chase, priced four ways.

Route to Caesars Diamond (15,000 Tier Credits)Coin-inEdgeExpected loss

Caesars pays 1 Tier Credit per $5 on reel slots and 1 per $10 on video poker, so video poker needs twice the coin-in for the same status — and still costs about a ninth as much, because the edge it charges you is seventeen times smaller. The one-day rows use Caesars' own daily Tier Credit bonus: earn 5,000 credits inside one promotional day and it adds 10,000, which is Diamond that day. Nothing changes but the scheduling.

Two catches the table cannot show. Time: $50,000 through a machine in one day is about 67 hours at $1.25 a hand and a bit over three hours at $25 a hand, so the cheap one-day route is a high-denomination play, not a quarter one. Skill: the 0.46% only exists if you play the hand right, and that is what the video poker trainer drills. Play video poker like a slot machine and you are back in the $6,000 column.

Even at $230 this is money spent on status, not a way to come out ahead: Diamond waives resort fees and feeds you once a year, so price that against whichever column applies to you before you chase it. Earn rates, tier bands and the daily bonus chart read at caesars.com/myrewards/earn-and-redeem on 2026-09-23; Caesars notes some machines earn at lower rates and are marked as such. 8% is the reel-slot hold the comps guide uses; 0.46% is the house edge of 9/6 Jacks or Better with optimal play (Shackleford, wizardofodds.com). The 600-hands-an-hour pace behind the time figures is a common estimate, not a published number — time your own.
