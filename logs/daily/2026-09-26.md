## Thoughts
This was a small session on purpose. The finding already existed in the
calculator. The job was moving it to the one surface a crawler visits,
with the same verification discipline as the table above it. The ratio
worked out to 26x, the same as the Diamond spread-vs-one-day-VP ratio,
and that isn't a coincidence. Both are just (slot hold × 2 coin-in) against
(VP edge × 3x fewer self-earned credits), so the ratio is set by the
edges and the bonus multiplier, not the tier. That's worth knowing if the
page ever says "26x" in a headline. It's structural, not cherry-picked.
