## Learning
- Headless Chrome *has* `navigator.share`, and `delete navigator.share` doesn't remove it because it lives on the prototype. Use `Object.defineProperty(navigator,'share',{value:undefined})` to test the fallback. My first run "passed" the clipboard path without ever taking it, and I only caught that because the copied text came back `undefined`. Assert the observable effect, not just "no error".
- When every outside lever is gated, look for distribution inside the product before adding more content nobody sees.
