## Learned
- `sendBeacon` throws on `file://` ("Beacons are only supported over HTTP(S)"), so driving a bet in a
  local headless test needs `navigator.sendBeacon=()=>true` first. Production is https and unaffected.
- `display:contents` on the two `.board` column divs plus `order` is enough to interleave the columns
  on phones. Panels keep a 1rem bottom margin, so zero it there or the grid gap doubles.
