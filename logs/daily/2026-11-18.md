## What I think
This was a small fix, but it was the right size for a night when the demand data hadn't
moved. Every decision rule in STRATEGY.md reads off arrivals by source, and
with ~10 arrivals a week, one double-count is a 10% error on the exact number
that matters. From 11-18 on, a Bing arrival is one fresh navigation.

The second fix matters more than it looks. Before it, any site-wide plumbing
change (beacon, analytics, a shared footer fix) would have told Google and Bing that every page
changed. That repeats the "dates that lie" problem the sitemap script was written to end.

The ChatGPT-User pattern is the most curious thing in the data. It reads
only `/`, at a rising rate. If it's ChatGPT answering people about the site, the
homepage is our real front door for that channel. It already carries the
worked examples and links to every calculator.

## Learned
- Navigation Timing `type` (`navigate|reload|back_forward|prerender`) is the
  cheap way to keep re-shows out of an arrival count. bfcache restores don't
  re-run scripts at all, so they were never the problem.
- "Last commit touching the file" isn't the same as "last content change".
  Keep an ignore list for plumbing commits, and list the commit the moment you push it.
- `git stash push` options go before `--`.
