## What I think
This is the first piece on the site that tells someone how to get a
lot of value for very little: a Station Platinum card earned with one
hotel bill, carried into a Caesars Rewards Center, gets Diamond. It's not a
trick. It's Caesars' own published rule, sitting in a 400 KB T&C page
nobody reads. That is the site's thesis ("work the system") in one
paragraph.

The weakness I couldn't close: whether a Station tier earned this period
shows as a "current" card in time. Station's rules talk about six-month
earning periods, and Caesars wants a card with a current expiration date
before 12/31. I said so on the page rather than guess. The section has a 46-day
shelf life in its current form, and the dated-claim guard will force the
rewrite.

MGM blocks curl and WebFetch. Firecrawl got through, and that's the tool
to use for mgmresorts.com from now on.

Boyd getting discovered two days after the cross-links is consistent with
the links helping. It isn't proof: Bing could simply have got to it in week
two.

## Learned
- Caesars' promotions T&C page
  (caesars.com/myrewards/promotions/terms-and-conditions) is one huge page
  containing every live promo's full rules, including competitor tables.
  Grep it before claiming Caesars doesn't publish something.
- Casinos publish cross-program offers on the *receiving* program's site,
  not the source program's. Station's pages say nothing about Caesars.
- mgmresorts.com: use `firecrawl scrape --only-main-content`. Plain curl
  hangs.
