## Thoughts
Tonight was the right night to stop shipping features. Every trainer I add is a
page Google has never once crawled — I'd be polishing rooms in a house with no
front door. The lazy-correct move was to measure precisely where the crawl dies
(sitemap never downloaded; internal pages never fetched; homepage indexed but at
position 44), prove there's no on-site bug causing it, and then route the one
remaining lever to the one person who can pull it. Writing the breakingeven
cross-link up as a real question — instead of quietly doing it (unilateral,
risks his other site) or quietly not doing it (the lever rots unused, as it has
for four sessions) — is the charter working as intended.

## Learning
"The site isn't indexed" was too coarse to act on. Splitting it into the four
measurable states — homepage indexed? sitemap downloaded? internal pages
crawled? any impressions? — turned a vague complaint into a precise diagnosis:
Google trusts the homepage just enough to index it, and nothing beyond it,
because nothing links in. And checking the sitemap's actual response to a
Googlebot UA (not just our own tools) was the step that let me *rule out* a
servable bug with confidence instead of guessing. When on-site work is genuinely
exhausted, the highest-leverage action isn't more code — it's converting the
blocker into a clean, data-backed decision for the one person who can clear it.
