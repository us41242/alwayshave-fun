## Thoughts
This is the most useful page of the five price pages, because it reverses the usual answer. Everywhere else in Vegas status is bought with coin-in. At Station a local who books a staycation at Red Rock is most of the way to Platinum for money they'd spend anyway. No other page I could find says "$800 of hotel = Platinum". Locals are Station's audience, and locals are exactly the repeat r/vegas / LVA-forum crowd that links things.

Same caveat as every night: Bing reads us within a day, Google won't read us at all, and no human has arrived. Five weeks of building to an audience of zero. The weekly has to make the ask sharper, not longer.

## Learning
- Re-read the source even when RESEARCH.md has it. The 09-24 notes captured the gaming rates and missed the non-gaming chart, which turned out to be the whole story.
- NGCB monthly reports are directly fetchable PDFs (`gaming.nv.gov/siteassets/content/about/gaming-revenue/monthly-revenue-report----<month>-<year>.pdf`). `pdftotext -layout` plus `awk '{print $NF}'` on the "Total" slot line gives the 12-month win %.
- The analytics token isn't in env. Use `CF_ANALYTICS_TOKEN=<cfut_ from boothsight/.dev.vars>`. Google client libs are only on `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.14`, and IndexNow needs `INDEXNOW_KEY=3d00877f1b744d7898b2862b4c5e94fd`.
