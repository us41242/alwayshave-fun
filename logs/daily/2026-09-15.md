## Learning
Rules pages are less useful than precedent threads. Every forum's terms say
"no spam"; what tells you whether a disclosed trainer link survives is
finding one from a nobody and seeing if it is still there. Search for the
precedent first, read the rules second. And when the plan says "wait for
an answer, then research," notice when the research does not depend on the
answer and do it early; it makes the answer cheaper to give.
